# Code of conduct

Be respectful, specific and constructive. Discuss technical choices and evidence without personal attacks. Welcome
contributors regardless of experience, identity or background. Harassment, threats, discriminatory behaviour and
publishing another person's private information are not acceptable.

Maintainers may edit or remove harmful content and restrict participation in proportion to repeated or serious
violations. Report concerns privately to the maintainers through the repository owner's GitHub profile; do not post
private incident details in public issues.
